# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

VL.Qt is used by artists, designers, programmers, technicians and studios, from the first patch to a running installation. We want everyone in this community to take part without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. A question about a node, a pin or a patch is always welcome, however basic it is. Many of us came to Qt, C# or vvvv from somewhere else.
- Assume good faith. Many of us write in a language that is not our first one, and short messages sound harsher than they are meant.
- Criticize the work, not the person. Say what does not work and how you reproduced it; a bug report is a gift.
- Give credit, accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect that people work in different settings: a hobby patch, a client project with a deadline, a long-running installation. Do not demand a fix on your schedule; maintainers decide what goes into VL.Qt and when.
- Be responsible for what you submit. A pull request, issue or comment written with the help of a coding agent is held to the same standard as one written by hand, and the person who posts it answers for it.

## What we do not accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or an email, without their explicit permission.
- Spam, advertising and unrelated promotion in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all spaces of the project: issues, pull requests, reviews and comments in this repository, and in public spaces where someone represents VL.Qt, such as a post on the vvvv forum on its behalf.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit, hide, lock or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and ban people from the repository, when they see conduct that breaks it. They explain the reason when they can do so without exposing the person who reported.

Report unacceptable behavior privately by mail to sebl@schnellebuntebilder.de, subject "VL.Qt conduct", or use "Report content" on GitHub for a single comment. All reports are reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and safety are respected. Please include links or screenshots, if you have them.

### Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in this order, as a guide:

1. **Correction.** Conduct judged unprofessional or unwelcome: a private message, with an explanation of why, and a request for a public apology where it fits. *Consequence:* none beyond the conversation.
2. **Warning.** A violation in one incident or a series of actions. *Consequence:* a warning with consequences for continued behavior; no interaction with the people involved, including the maintainers, for a set time. Breaking these terms can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** A serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behavior. *Consequence:* a temporary ban from all interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** A pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups. *Consequence:* a permanent ban from all interaction with the project.

A person can ask the maintainers to review a decision by answering the mail in which it was communicated.
